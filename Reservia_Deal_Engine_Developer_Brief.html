1. Purpose and scope

Reservia needs one commercial engine that takes a lead from the CRM to a priced, approved proposal, then to a project budget in the ERP once the deal is won. It covers four sectors: on-grid commercial and industrial, off-grid, agriculture pumping, and utility scale.

The engine has to do five things:

  1. Hold a Price Book of supplier prices, updated daily, with a flag for stale prices.
  2. Build the bill of quantities (BOQ) for each sector from the design inputs, then price installation, transport, travel and personnel.
  3. Apply the pricing stack (overhead, contingency, profit, sales bonus, engineering incentive) and produce a price per kWp for the whole offer.
  4. Run a two-stage approval: Stage 1 signs off gross profit, Stage 2 signs off the allocations (sales bonus, engineering incentive, overhead, contingency, profit and category addition) and only opens after Stage 1. The MD signs when net price excl VAT is above EGP 1.5M. Release the proposal only when every required sign-off is in.
  5. Pass the won budget to the ERP, and report proposals, timing, won rate and production by sector.

Out of scope for Rev 00: the payroll, the balance sheet and the P&L (these sit in the financial model), and the website.

3.2 Site costs

  • Size band: 1 (up to 15 kWp), 2 (up to 30), 3 (up to 50), 4 (up to 100), 5 (above 100).
  • Installation and testing = kWp x rate, where rate = Rates matrix [region, band]. Transport = Rates matrix [region, band] (lump sum).
  • Greater Cairo (Cairo - 10th of Ramadan) has no travel, hotel or per diem. Elsewhere: fares = crew x fare; room-nights = ceil(crew / 2) x days; hotels = room-nights x rate; per diem = crew x days x EGP 250 (Reservia rule).
  • Personnel allowance = crew x days x EGP 300 per person-day (ASSUMPTION).

3.4 Customer feasibility

  • Capex = final price incl VAT (year 0). Yield year n = kWp x yield x (1 - degradation)^(n - 1), default yield 1,800 kWh/kWp, degradation 0.7%.
  • Market tariff year n = tariff x (1 + escalation)^(n - 1). Tariff 2.80 EGP/kWh, escalation 10% a year. Avoided cost = yield x tariff.
  • Simple payback = first year where cumulative cash turns positive, interpolated. IRR over 25 years (year 0 to 25). NPV = -capex + sum of savings discounted at the discount rate (22%).
  • Levelised solution cost per kWh = capex / sum of 25-year yield (flat).
  • O&M is included in the EPC offer, so no O&M is deducted from savings.

3.5 ERP budget

Budget lines are the BOQ groups (equipment, structures, cabling, protection, balance of system, utility adders), site costs (installation, transport, travel, personnel), overhead, contingency, sales bonus and engineering incentive. Profit is excluded. Check: budget less (COGS + overhead + contingency + incentives) must equal zero.

4. Approval workflow

Stage 1Ops & Sales Manager and Technical leadEvery dealSigns off gross profit against the floor (15%, ASSUMPTION) and the technical scope

MDManaging DirectorNet price excl VAT above EGP 1,500,000Final sign-off

Release status = APPROVED FOR ISSUE only when Stage 1 is Approved with GP above the floor, Stage 2 is Approved, and the MD is Approved where required. Every status change is logged with user, time and value (the demo records names and dates only; see section 4.1). Proposals cannot be sent while the status reads NOT RELEASED.

4.1 Routing and sign-off record

Each stage has an approver name, a sign-off date and a note, entered next to its status. The Pricing tab shows a "Waiting on" line that names the next sign-off: Stage 1 approver, then Stage 2 approver, then the MD where net is above EGP 1.5M. If a stage is marked Approved without a name or date, the tab flags it. Rejected stages say to revise the pricing or allocations.

What the demo does not do: it does not check who is logged in. Anyone who can open the file can enter any name and set any status. Access control by role needs the CRM or ERP backend.

6. Integrations

6.1 CRM to engine (build in CRM Phase 2)

  • Trigger: CRM lead stage changes to Proposal. Send a POST to the engine with the deal payload below. Idempotent on crm_lead_id.
  • Engine creates a Deal and a RegisterRow (outcome Open), prefilled from the lead, and returns the deal_id.
  • Stage Won: CRM sends an outcome event. Engine sets outcome Won, creates ERPBudgetLine rows, and calls the ERP push.
  • Stage Lost: CRM sends an outcome event with a reason (required). Engine sets outcome Lost.

Example payload (Proposal trigger):

{ "crm_lead_id": "CRM-00001", "stage": "Proposal", "stage_changed_at": "2026-10-28T10:00:00+02:00",
  "lead_created_at": "2026-10-20", "customer": "Sample Customer", "sector": "On-Grid C&I", "sub_type": "Factory",
  "region": "Cairo - 10th of Ramadan", "kwp": 80, "annual_kwh": 155000, "source": "web|whatsapp|call|referral" }

6.2 Engine to ERP (build)

  • On Won: POST project budget. Body = erp_project_code, customer, lines (cost_code, line, budget_egp), total_budget_egp, won_date. Retry with backoff; record the ERP project id back on the deal.
  • Profit is never sent. Budget total equals cost lines only (see section 3.5).

6.3 Analytics

  • Amplitude events already named in the CRM spec (crm_quote_sent, crm_quote_accepted, crm_quote_declined, crm_deal_won, crm_deal_lost). Add: proposal_released (after Stage 1, Stage 2 and MD where required), price_book_updated (daily), approval_decided (stage, status).

6.4 Price Book daily update

  • Rev 00 uses a manual daily update (the price owner enters prices and check dates). Build a CSV import (same columns as the Price Book sheet) with a preview and per-line errors, matching the CRM import pattern.
  • Staleness rule: an item older than one day shows RECHECK PRICE. An item with status not Available shows CHECK AVAILABILITY. Blocked from proposal release if any BOQ line uses a flagged item (confirm this rule with Ahmed).
